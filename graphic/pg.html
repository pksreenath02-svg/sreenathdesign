Pergamon

Self-initiated

What if the countdown repaired the frieze?

The Pergamon Altar has been hidden for years. Instead of counting days, this campaign counts damage: every poster has fewer cracks, until the gods are whole on opening day.

  • Art direction

Deliverables

Next project
Brief

Self-set: a reopening campaign concept for Berlin's Pergamonmuseum.

Audience

Berliners and visitors who have waited years to see the altar again.

Constraint

One image across five posters; only the damage changes.

Result

Concept, not affiliated with the museum. It shows a countdown idea that holds in print, outdoor and web.
