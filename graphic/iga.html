IGA 2027

Self-initiated

Don’t hide the industry. Let it bloom.

IGA 2027 turns five former industrial sites into gardens. The campaign pairs a structure from each site with a flower: the headframe with a sunflower, the coking plant with cornflowers, the crane with a poppy, the canal with marsh marigolds, the slag heap with wild carrot.

Brief

Self-set: a campaign concept for IGA 2027, the garden exhibition planned across five Ruhr sites in 2027.

Audience

Families, day-trippers and locals across the Ruhr.

Constraint

Five sites, one identity, readable from a tram stop or across a station hall.

Result

Concept, not commissioned. It shows one idea carried through posters, wayfinding, digital and motion.
