GRAPHIC DESIGN · 04/05

Making

A conceptual editorial magazine exploring how design and material honesty shape tomorrow's world.

CategoryGraphic Design
RoleEditorial Design & Art Direction

MAKING is a self-directed editorial project — each issue built around a single guiding question about design and culture. Issue 02 asks: “How do today's designers shape tomorrow's world?”

An organic blob-shaped graphic system contrasts with a disciplined editorial grid; the issue explores its theme through the lens of material honesty — timber, stone, paper, concrete — using full-bleed photography and restrained serif/sans typography.

A cohesive editorial identity that treats the magazine itself as a design object, not just a container for articles.
